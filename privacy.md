# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

Drumshift comes in two editions. What they collect differs, so each is described separately.

## Your files — both editions

- All MIDI conversion happens locally on your computer. No file you open is uploaded anywhere.
- Converted files are saved next to the original, or to your `Music/Drumshift` folder.
- The app keeps a local list of recent conversions and your layout choices in its own preferences. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.
- Drumshift has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Mac App Store edition

The edition downloaded from the Mac App Store does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It has no accounts and makes no network connections.

## Edition downloaded from mirablvck.com (Mac and Windows)

This edition is free and asks you to sign in once with your email address.

- **What is collected:** your email address, a display name if you choose to add one, and the date you first signed in.
- **Why:** to create your MIRABLVCK account, which holds your free Drumshift license, and to send you the 6-digit sign-in code. MIRABLVCK may also email you about its products; every such email will let you opt out.
- **Where it goes:** the app connects to `sigil.mirablvck.com` when you sign in and, when you are online, when it starts, to confirm the account is still valid. Nothing about your files or how you use the app is sent.
- **Where it is kept:** on MIRABLVCK's account server, hosted by Cloudflare. The sign-in session is stored on your computer, protected by the operating system.
- **Your choices:** sign out at any time from the app. To see your account, go to [mirablvck.com/account](https://mirablvck.com/account). To have your account and email deleted, write to [iam@mirablvck.com](mailto:iam@mirablvck.com).
