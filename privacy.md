# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

Drumshift does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- All MIDI conversion happens locally on your Mac. No file you open is uploaded anywhere.
- Converted files are saved to `~/Music/Drumshift` on your Mac.
- The app keeps a local list of recent conversions and your layout choices in its own preferences. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.
- Drumshift has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and makes no network connections.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/mirosblack/drumshift/issues/new).
